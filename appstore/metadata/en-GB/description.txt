A tiny calendar that lives in your menu bar. Click the icon, see the month, click away. That's the whole app.

If you've used Windows, it's the calendar you get by clicking the clock in the taskbar: a quick look at the month and nothing else.

• Today is highlighted, and the week starts on the day your region settings say.
• Click the month name to see the whole year, or the year to pick another one.
• Arrow keys move the selected day. Page Up and Page Down, the scroll wheel or the arrow buttons flip through the months.
• Follows light and dark mode.
• Asks once whether to open at login. Right-click the icon to change your mind.
• Speaks English, German, French, Spanish, Dutch and Danish.

What it leaves out, on purpose: no events, no accounts, no settings window, no permissions to grant. It collects nothing and never goes online.
