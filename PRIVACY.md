# Privacy Policy

MenuBarCalendar collects no data.

- It never connects to the internet.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
- It does not read your calendars, contacts, files or location.
- It remembers one thing on your Mac: whether it has already asked you about opening at login. That setting stays in the app's own preferences on your Mac and is never sent anywhere.

If that ever changes, this page will change first.

Questions go in an issue at https://github.com/christianjbrown/macos-menu-bar-calendar/issues.
